# oo_v1 Reverse-GNSS Simulation — Realism Audit (v4): Optimistic vs Conservative Assumptions

**Reviewer role:** Senior MATLAB developer / GNSS & Kalman-filter specialist
**Branch audited:** `feature/scientific-correctness-v2` (HEAD `56d4675`, working tree clean apart from `output/ladder_3600s.csv`)
**Date:** 2026-07-14
**Scope:** A single question, applied to every numeric assumption in the pipeline — *are the noise sigmas, biases, error magnitudes, process-noise (Q), initial covariance (P0), and modelling choices physically representative of a real one-way ≥5-tower GEO reverse-GNSS system estimating position + clock + attitude, or are they too OPTIMISTIC (over-confident / unrealistically small error) or too CONSERVATIVE (needlessly pessimistic)?*
**Relationship to v1–v3:** v1/v2/v3 established the *internal* correctness — the error chain does **not** double-count any effect, and truth is cleanly separated from estimation. This v4 assumes those results and audits **realism only**. It does not re-litigate double-counting or separation.
**Method:** A 7-aspect multi-agent audit (Kalman-filter logic · measurement noise · error stream · clock · frames · diagnostics · systemic estimation), each aspect independently analysed and then **adversarially re-verified** (14 senior-reviewer passes, ~1.07 M tokens), then synthesised. Every quantitative claim was additionally grounded by the lead reviewer against the supplied reference PDFs (JOW *Modeling & Simulating GNSS…* Table 2.1; Kaplan & Hegarty) and against the project's own empirical scenario-ladder output (`output/ladder_3600s.csv`). Source-level code-bug claims (H11, M8) were verified at file:line. See **Appendix E** for that independent grounding.

## 0. Executive summary

This is the v4 **realism** audit of the oo_v1 one-way, 5-tower, single-GEO reverse-GNSS simulation. It presumes the v3 result that the error chain does **not** double-count and that truth/estimation separation is clean; it asks only one question: *are the sigmas, biases, process-noise, initial covariance, and modelling choices physically representative of a real system?* The answer is that the default ("headline") configuration is **systematically and pervasively optimistic**, and the celebrated sub-100 ps clock / sub-wavelength position/attitude regime is an artefact of several stacked idealisations rather than an achievable result. Genuinely *conservative* (needlessly pessimistic) settings are rare and minor — the asymmetry is itself the headline finding.

> **Empirical corroboration (Appendix E).** The project's own scenario ladder (`output/ladder_3600s.csv`, 3600 s runs) already shows the honest picture: the baseline one-way ground fix reaches **radial 10.2 m / clock 10.3 m (≈34 ns)**, a 5-towers-only geometry reaches **64 m radial/clock**, and the sub-100 ps regime (0.012 m ≈ 40 ps, rung `15`) appears **only** with the ISL swarm. The sub-100 ps headline is therefore not produced by the one-way ground segment at all — it is inherited from aiding paths (ISL/two-way) and/or the idealisations below.

### CONSERVATIVE (needlessly pessimistic) issues — few and minor

- Higher-order ionosphere charged to R at **full unmodelled magnitude** (2nd-order ≤5 cm, 3rd-order ≤5 mm) — correct but conservative; keep.

- A white SNC sized to the *full* 7e-6 m/s² luni-solar magnitude would be over-conservative over seconds (the honest fix is a Gauss-Markov acceleration state, not a bigger white σ).
- `sigma_angAccel=1e-7 rad/s²` sits at the **conservative end** of the Wertz GEO torque budget — keep.

No high-severity conservative finding exists; the tuning error is one-directional.

## 2. Per-aspect realism verdicts

### 2.1 EKF core logic & tuning (Q, P0, dynamics)

**Verdict: structurally sound filter, but the dynamics are unrealistically easy.** The Joseph-form update, MEKF error-state reset, and Brown-Hwang clock-Q mapping are all correct (independently checked). The realism failure is upstream of the filter: **truth and EKF share the identical pure-J2 propagator** (masterConfig.m:425-428) with `modelMismatch.enable=false`, so the estimator faces *zero* force-model error. This matters because a J2-only model omits luni-solar acceleration (Moon 2·GM_m·r_geo/d_m³ = 7.3e-6 m/s², Sun 3.3e-6 m/s²) that is *comparable in magnitude to J2 itself* (~8.3e-6 m/s² at GEO) — the dominant post-J2 GEO perturbation and the driver of the ~0.85°/yr inclination drift. `sigma_accel=1e-6 m/s²` (baseConfig.m:219) is then a covariance sized to a perturbation-free truth: harmless in the shipped default (produces zero actual error), but ~7× too small the moment truth perturbations are added. The honest fix is a **first-order Gauss-Markov acceleration state (τ≈1 sidereal day)** since luni-solar is quasi-DC, not white — a full-magnitude white SNC at 7e-6 would be over-conservative over seconds and under-conservative over the diurnal period (H3, M1). `P0_omega=1e-12 rad/s` (L9) is cosmetically over-tight but inert (ω frozen at truth 0). *Ref: Montenbruck & Gill, Satellite Orbits Table 3.1; Tapley/Schutz/Born SNC sizing.*

### 2.2 Measurement noise sigmas & R construction

**Verdict: the individual thermal sigmas are mostly defensible; the R construction is optimistic through omitted correlations and disabled weighting.** The largest defect is **H1**: the tower broadcast-clock product σ=0.01 m ≈ 33 ps (masterConfig.m:145) is common-mode on every code row from a tower and maps almost directly into the receiver-clock covariance, yet 33 ps is IGS-final post-processed accuracy applied at real-time 5 s latency / 30 s cadence — broadcast (~5 ns) / ultra-rapid-predicted (~3 ns) / IGS-RTS (~0.1–0.15 ns) are the right anchors, so it is optimistic by 0.5–2 orders. Secondary: code σ is `constant` (M7) — for a near-stationary GEO this is a *spatial* across-tower mis-weighting (low-elevation towers with the largest true thermal+residual-tropo error are over-trusted); the 1 mm `sigmaFloor` (L3) is non-physical but dormant at 0.30 m; the two carrier-noise fields disagree 5 mm vs 1.9 mm (L4); tower-clock correlation is omitted on carrier/Doppler (L5, bounded <2× on absolute rows, cancels in single-differenced attitude). The base thermal values (0.30/0.45 m code, 5 mm carrier, 0.01 m/s Doppler) are individually credible — their only sin is sitting below the codebase's *own* real-world guards (0.60/0.90 m, 0.010 m, 0.03 m/s), i.e. a scenario-labelling issue (L1, L2). *Ref: Kaplan & Hegarty DLL/FLL noise; RTKLIB elevation model; IGS product accuracy tables.*

### 2.3 Truth-side error stream (atmosphere, biases, multipath)

**Verdict: the atmosphere realism upgrade is good, but three first-order systematics are simply absent from truth.** The Saastamoinen/Niell troposphere, diurnal/Klobuchar/topside ionosphere, higher-order iono, and scintillation GM are all live and well-parameterised in the default realism run. But **code multipath is off** (H4: a 0.30 m / 60 s GM ≈ 1 ns of code error the EKF cannot average, ~10× a 100 ps budget), **all differential code biases are zero** (H5: real P1-P2 DCBs run ±10 ns; the per-tower differential and per-frequency parts leak into iono/clock even though the common-mode receiver part is absorbed by b_rx), and **hardware group delay is inert** (M2: per-tower differential 0.5–5 ns). PCV (M3, uncalibrated 1–2 cm, does *not* common-mode-cancel between differently-oriented attitude antennas), carrier wind-up (M4, 19 cm/rev but largely common-mode + ambiguity-absorbed), solid-Earth tide (H7), and tower survey error (L8) are all omitted. The ZWD mean of ~7.5 cm (L7) is dry-side but absorbed by the estimated per-tower ZWD state; Klobuchar 0.75 skill (L6) is optimistic but dormant under the dual-frequency default. **Caveat against double-counting:** the frequency-dependent part of hardware delay *is* the DCB — inject the broadband per-tower delay (M2) *or* the differential DCB (H5), not both at full magnitude. *Ref: Kaplan & Hegarty §7.2; IGS DCB / Schaer; IERS Conventions 2010 Ch.7; Wu et al. 1993; Schmid et al. 2007.*

### 2.4 Clock realism

**Verdict: the single most important realism failure in the whole simulation.** `templateSource='legacy'` (masterConfig.m:140) selects `CESIUM1 h0=1e-26` (ConfigFactory.m:2000), giving σ_y(1 s)=sqrt(h0/2)=**7.1e-14** — *better than an active H-maser* and 2–3 orders quieter than a real caesium (5071A σ_y(1 s)≈5–8.5e-12 → h0≈1e-22; JOW generic Cs h0=1e-19). The self-labelled comment (masterConfig.m:133-137) admits this is "idealised/optimistic". This propagates deterministically into the EKF clock-Q: sqrt(Q₁₁)=c·sqrt(h0/2)=0.071 ps/epoch (legacy) vs 7 ps (5071A) vs 224 ps (JOW) — the filter believes the clock is frozen between 1 s updates, so clock covariance/NEES are ~3 decades over-confident (C1, H2). The Brown-Hwang Q *structure* is correct (verified against IEEE-1139 RWFM/WFM); the defect is entirely the h-parameter input. Compounding: **deterministic tower clocks** (M6) mean the linear broadcast prediction is exact except for injected noise, so the R-inflation covers wander the truth never contains; the **relativistic clock term is off and the truth orbit is exactly circular** (M5), so the physically real 0.29–0.86 ns periodic term at e=1e-4–3e-4 is never tested. **Above all (C2):** every cited sub-100 ps result (Merlo&Nanzer 2.26 ps, SDR 0.7 ps, T2L2 ~50 ps, EM-WaTT ~100 ps) uses two-way reciprocal cancellation; one-way estimation is legitimate but reaches only ~0.3–30 ns clock accuracy for this geometry. *Ref: JOW Table 2.1; IEEE Std 1139-2008; Riley/NIST SP1065; Ashby 2003; Brown & Hwang App. B.*

### 2.5 Frames, light-time, Sagnac

**Verdict: the geometry mechanics are correct; the frame *realisation* is an oracle.** Light-time (2 iterations @ 1e-12 s, residual ~2e-13 s), the ω·τ Sagnac path, and Shapiro (18.2 mm, matched both sides) are all verified correct and non-optimistic. The realism gap is that the Earth-orientation model is a **constant-Ω rotation with no EOP/UT1/polar motion/nutation/precession** (H6) and **no station-displacement tides** (H7). These produce *zero* in-sim error only because the identical map is applied to truth and model — for a real ITRF/ICRF deployment the *post-correction residuals* (cm–dm range, 0.1–1 ns clock from EOP; mm–cm from tides after dehanttideinel) do not cancel and set a floor below which the sub-100 ps claim is meaningless. The full uncorrected magnitudes are large (polar motion Rₑ·x_p ≈ 9.3 m; solid-Earth tide ~0.30 m radial > λ_L1), which is why they are mandatory IERS-2010 terms. `FrameTimeUtils` itself documents the limitation: *"appropriate for metre-to-sub-metre navigation… For IERS-grade timing (< 1 ns)… full EOP products are needed."* The pseudo-inertial anchoring (L13) and lever-arm scalar attitude σ (L12) are labelling/reporting refinements. *Ref: IERS Conventions 2010 Ch.5,7; Kaplan & Hegarty (Sagnac ~30 m).*

### 2.6 Diagnostics & consistency-metric trustworthiness

**Verdict: several consistency statistics do not measure what they claim, so the "filter is consistent" verdict is partly unearned.** The clear bug is **H11**: `NEES_pos` is stored already divided by its 3 dof (SimulationDataStore.m:829), then the MC pool re-counts 3 dof per sample (MonteCarloConsistency.m:74), so the pooled statistic collapses to E[·]≈0.33 and produces a spurious "below-band / CONSERVATIVE" verdict from a units error (fix: `dofNEES += sum(gE)`). This is the direct source of the v3-reported "NEES/dof≈0.36". Additionally: the **attitude NEES uses a raw Euler difference** instead of the small-angle error-state that P(euler_idx) actually covers (M8, correct routine is dead code — bias bounded but grows near gimbal lock); the **differential-attitude update's innovation is discarded** so the attitude measurement model (on by default) is never consistency-tested (M9); the headline NIS mixes gauge-augmented numerator with physical-row dof (L10, dormant at default); and a **single deterministic run is graded against a fixed χ² [0.5,2] band** with MC off (M10) — with a filter time constant of hundreds of seconds a 14400 s run yields only tens of effective samples, so the band is too tight to justify the pass/warn claim. The headline also leads with **matched-atmosphere final-20-epoch RMS** (L11) rather than the honest whole-run/mismatched figures the code already computes. *Ref: Bar-Shalom, Li & Kirubarajan 2001 §5.4; Markley & Crassidis 2014 MEKF.*

### 2.7 Systemic estimation realism

**Verdict: the headline numbers are structurally borrowed, not measured.** Three coupled structural facts explain why the result looks better than physically achievable. (1) **Radial↔clock is geometrically degenerate** (H10): asin(Rₑ/R_g)=8.70°, so all tower LOS lie within 8.7° of radial and a clock bias vs a radial shift differ only at (1−cos 8.7°)=0.0115 → **87× amplification** of that mode. Wide/many towers *reduce* it (empirically 5 towers→64 m radial vs the wider default→10 m — Appendix E), but it never fully separates from one ground hemisphere. (2) Because tower geometry cannot fully split radial from clock, the **truth-matched J2 orbit** (H3) is the other thing pinning radial, hence clock — the clock accuracy is partly inherited from an idealised orbit model, not purely from timing observables. (3) The **attitude solution is handed the answer**: differential carrier biases are calibrated at the 0.1°-noised *truth* attitude (H8) and inter-antenna biases are declared `syntheticKnownZero` (H6/H9) — real inter-antenna biases (~0.1–0.5 cycle, temperature-drifting) are the single hardest term in GPS attitude AR, and zeroing them lets the integer search fix trivially. Add one-way + quiet clock (C1/C2) and the sub-100 ps / sub-wavelength headline is the product of ~six stacked idealisations. *Ref: Kaplan & Hegarty (DOP/geometry); Montenbruck & Gill; Cohen 1992; Teunissen 2006 (LAMBDA).*

## 3. Realistic accuracy envelope

Given honest, de-optimised assumptions (realistic caesium clock, one-way geometry, a truth≠EKF force gap, injected multipath/DCB/hardware delay, unknown inter-antenna biases, EOP/tide residuals), the physically defensible expectation for **this specific one-way, 5-tower, single-GEO reverse-GNSS architecture** is:

| Quantity | Current headline | Honest realistic envelope | Dominant limiter |
|----------|------------------|---------------------------|------------------|
| **Receiver clock (bias)** | sub-100 ps | **~0.3–30 ns** (ns-class). Free-run wander alone ~27 ns/4 h with JOW Cs, ~few ns with 5071A; the estimated bias floors on the ground reference + radial coupling. | One-way (no reciprocal cancellation, C2) + realistic clock Q (C1) + tower product σ 0.1–1 ns (H1). |
| **Position — radial** | sub-wavelength / sub-m | **several m to tens of m** (≈87× the horizontal error). | Geometric radial↔clock degeneracy (H10) + force-model gap once truth≠EKF (H3). Dynamics-limited. |
| **Position — horizontal** | sub-wavelength | **~sub-m to a few m** (scales ~1/√N_towers with code/carrier noise). | Code multipath+DCB (H4/H5), elevation weighting (M7). |
| **Attitude** | sub-0.1° | **~0.1° to ~1°**, worst on the boresight axis (~5× the in-plane axes, L12). | Truth-anchored calibration removed (H8) + unknown inter-antenna biases (H9) + scintillation-inflated carrier σ 0.02–0.05 cyc. |

The headline is achievable **only** with two-way reciprocal transfer *and* an idealised-maser clock *and* a truth-matched orbit *and* a truth-anchored attitude reference **simultaneously**. A configuration that is honest on any one of clock, transfer geometry, or dynamics already leaves the sub-100 ps regime. This is corroborated empirically by the scenario ladder (Appendix E).

## Appendix E — Independent lead-reviewer grounding (PDF + empirical + source verification)

This appendix records the checks the lead reviewer ran outside the multi-agent audit, to keep v4 from resting on model assertions alone.

### E.1 Reference-PDF anchors (text-extracted this pass)

- **Clock (JOW, *Modeling & Simulating GNSS Signal Structures and Receivers*, Table 2.1, p.100).** Exact values confirmed: **Cesium1 h0=1×10⁻¹⁹, h₋₁=1×10⁻²⁵, h₋₂=2×10⁻³²**; **OCXO2 h0=2.51×10⁻²⁶**. The code's `legacy` CESIUM1 (`ConfigFactory.m:2000`) uses h0=**1×10⁻²⁶** — 7 orders below JOW Cesium1, and *quieter than the code's own legacy OCXO (h0=2×10⁻²⁵)*, which is physically inverted (a real caesium beam has **worse** short-term stability than a good OCXO; it wins only at long τ). σ_y(1 s): legacy 7.1×10⁻¹⁴ vs JOW-Cs 2.2×10⁻¹⁰ (≈3160×) vs HP5071A ≈8.5×10⁻¹² (≈120×). Grounds **C1/H2** with the primary source.
- **Carrier/code noise (Kaplan & Hegarty, *Understanding GPS*, §5.6).** λ_L1 = 0.1903 m/cycle confirmed; the code's carrier σ = 5 mm (≈0.026 cyc) and the 0.01-cyc diagnostic floor (≈1.9 mm) are **thermal-realistic** for a high-C/N₀ space receiver — i.e. the base thermal sigmas are *not* the primary optimism (consistent with L1/L4 being labelling issues, not magnitude errors). The optimism is in the *disabled elevation weighting* (M7) and *omitted correlated errors* (H4/H5), not the thermal floor.

### E.2 Empirical accuracy envelope (`output/ladder_3600s.csv`, 3600 s runs)

The project's own scenario ladder is the strongest realism evidence and directly validates §3:

| Rung | radial_m | clock_m (→ time) | posRMS_m | meanNIS / measRows | Reading |
|------|----------|------------------|----------|--------------------|---------|
| `00_baseline` | 10.20 | 10.32 (**≈34 ns**) | 10.35 | 55.1 / 60 (**0.92**) | Honest one-way ground fix: **tens of ns, ~10 m radial** — not sub-100 ps. |

### E.3 Source-verified code-bug confirmations

- **H11 confirmed:** `SimulationDataStore.m:829` → `NEES_pos = (r_err'·(P_pos\r_err))/3` (per-dof, E≈1); `MonteCarloConsistency.m:74` → `dofNEES = dofNEES + 3*sum(gE)`. Pooling per-dof values against 3× dof gives neesPerDof ≈ 0.33. **This is the true origin of the v3 "NEES/dof≈0.36 → CONSERVATIVE" verdict** — it is largely a normalization artifact, not physical under-confidence. (The NIS side, dof = measurement rows, is computed correctly and its ~0.92 is real.)
- **M8 confirmed:** `SimulationDataStore.m:848` recomputes `NEES_att` from a raw wrapped-Euler difference (`:739`) rather than the EKF's quaternion-aware `attitudeSmallAngleError_`/`computeNEES` (`ReverseGNSSEKF.m:498-529`, which is correct but not on the reporting path). In quaternion-error-state mode the difference of two Euler triples is not the error in P(euler_idx) space.

### E.4 Net calibration note
